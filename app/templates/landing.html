Frequently asked questions

What is StudySphere?

StudySphere is an AI study platform for students. You upload your lecture notes or textbook extracts, then generate quizzes, flashcards and answers based on those notes, and track your progress over time.

What file types can I upload?

You can upload PDF, TXT and DOCX files up to 10 MB. StudySphere extracts the text so you can read it, search it and use it with the AI study tools.

Does the AI assistant answer from my notes?

Yes. The assistant is grounded in the document you select. When the answer is not in your notes, it says so instead of guessing.

Can I create quizzes and flashcards from my own notes?

Yes. Choose a document, then generate quizzes or flashcard decks. You can choose the number of questions and the difficulty, and quizzes include an explanation for each answer.

Is my study data private?

Your documents, quizzes, flashcards and progress belong to your account. Other users cannot see them.
